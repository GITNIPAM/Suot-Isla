- **Scope:** Full multi-page e-commerce storefront (`index.html`, `catalog.html`, `product.html`, `cart.html`, `checkout.html`, `auth.html`, `account.html`), role-based operations portal (`admin.html`), and architecture presentation (`diagrams.html`).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Philippine island surf & resort outfitters dispatch and storefront rooted in tactile coastal utility—refusing generic SaaS card grids and sterile white fast-fashion templates in favor of warm sand surfaces, deep lagoon ink, woven-stitch details, and live island series field gear.

OWN-WORLD: Warm Sand (`#F4EAD5`) and bleached linen (`#FAF6EE`) grounds paired with Deep Lagoon Teal (`#0E6B74`) primary actions and headers, deep ocean ink (`#092C30`), Sun-Bleached Coral (`#F27D63`) low-stock and promo tags, and Palm Leaf Green (`#2E5A44`) on Seafoam Mist (`#A8D5BA`) fulfillment pills, set in Plus Jakarta Sans Bold headings, DM Sans body, and JetBrains Mono tabular specs.

STORY: Visitors immediately recognize Suot Isla ("Island Wear") as authentic Philippine surf-town gear built for Siargao, Boracay, El Nido, and La Union, browse and filter all 16 products by destination and variant, and complete a full order-to-delivery-and-return lifecycle with live email notifications.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top coastal dispatch bar and sticky Lagoon-accented header featuring the circular woven-stitch stamp logo, Island Series navigation, Email Outbox trigger, and persistent Bag drawer; opening into an asymmetric editorial split showcasing the 4 Philippine Island Series alongside interactive quick-shop gear cards and primary "Explore the 16-Piece Catalog" CTA.
